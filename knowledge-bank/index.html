Mission

Build a high-safety AI chatbot that performs moderation, channel management, and user management from natural language while maintaining strict policy boundaries and audit trails.

Critical Constraint

Zero budget for first 3 months, 2-person team, enterprise-ready architecture path without overengineering v1.

Primary Risk

Incorrect high-stakes actions. Mitigation uses confidence gates, confirmations, policy engines, and autonomous kill-switch controls.

v1 Success

Working end-to-end app in ~90 days: OAuth, onboarding, chat-to-action, moderation intelligence, autonomy toggle, and full audit visibility.
